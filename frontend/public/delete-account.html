Delete your Sadhana Record account

You can delete your Sadhana Record account at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, what is kept, and how long it takes.

Delete it in the app

  1. Sign in to Sadhana Record.
  2. Open Account and go to Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete my account…, enter your password and type DELETE.

Your account is deleted at once, and you are signed out on every phone.

Coordinators, facilitators and IYF heads: ask the IYF head to remove your role first. Then you can delete your account in the same way.

If you can't sign in

  • Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. Once your coordinator approves the new password, sign in and delete your account as above.
  • Or email iyfdelhi.database@gmail.com from any email address with your registered phone number, and we will delete it for you.

What is deleted

  • Your account, login and password
  • Your profile (birthday, blood group, contact details, address, emergency contact) and your photo
  • Days you filled in but did not submit
  • Your comments and likes on announcements
  • The notifications set up for you on your phone

What is kept, and how

The weeks you submitted, your attendance and your missed weeks stay in your batch's records, so your batch's past totals don't change. They are kept without your name, phone number or IYF ID: they show as "Deleted devotee", and nothing in them links back to you. This applies in the app and in the Google Sheets copies of its data.

How long it takes

In the app
At once.
Google Sheets copies
Within about an hour.
Backups
The database's restore points last 7 days. Weekly backup files are kept for 8 weeks, plus one file a month; your details leave the backups as they expire. Backups are used only to recover from an accident.

The facilitator dashboard

The IYF facilitator dashboard is a separate system and may keep its own records of you. To have those removed, ask your facilitator or email iyfdelhi.database@gmail.com.
