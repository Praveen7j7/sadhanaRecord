Privacy policy

Sadhana Record is the app ISKCON Youth Forum, Delhi (IYF Delhi) uses for daily sadhana: you record your day, your batch sees how the week went. This page says what the app keeps about you, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

Draft for IYF's approval. Last updated 8 October 2026.

What we keep

  • Your account: mobile number (it is your login), name, password (stored only as a one-way hash, never readable), and whether the number is on WhatsApp.
  • Your profile: email, date of birth, blood group, address, an emergency contact, your batch, class day and facilitator. A photo only if you add one.
  • For members under 18: the name and mobile number of the parent or guardian who agreed to the sign-up.
  • Your sadhana: what you fill in each day and week (sleep and wake times, rounds, hearing, reading, seva, comments), your scores and attendance.
  • Announcements: your likes, comments and any reports you make.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the address your phone gives us to deliver them.
  • Security records: sign-ins and important changes (for example who approved an account), so mistakes and misuse can be traced.

We never ask for Aadhaar or any other identity document.

Why we keep it

  • To run your account and your batch's weekly sadhana record.
  • So your coordinator, facilitator and group leader can guide you and contact you.
  • To reach someone in an emergency at an IYF programme (blood group, emergency contact).
  • To send the reminders and announcements you allow.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or tracking services.

Who can see it

  • You: everything about yourself.
  • Your group leader: whether you filled and submitted your week, and your attendance.
  • Your facilitator: your sadhana and scores, and your phone number to contact you.
  • Your batch coordinator: your profile, contact details and sadhana, to approve and look after members.
  • The IYF head: everything, to run the app for all batches.
  • Other members: only your name, on the comments you post on announcements.

Where it is stored

The app's data is stored with Cloudflare (servers in the Asia-Pacific region). A copy is kept in Google Sheets that only IYF Delhi's coordinators and the IYF head can open, for their day-to-day work. Notifications are delivered through your phone's own service (Google or Apple).

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When an account is deleted, your profile, contacts, photo, unsent days, comments and likes are removed; the weeks you submitted stay in your batch's totals without your name, phone number or IYF ID. Details: delete your account.

Members under 18

You must be at least 15 to sign up. If you are 15, 16 or 17, a parent or guardian must agree to your sign-up, and we keep their name and number with your account.

Your rights

  • See and correct your details in Account (some are changed by your coordinator).
  • Delete your account at any time from Account, or by email.
  • Turn reminders off at any time from Account or your phone's settings.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or raise a concern, by email.

Contact

IYF Delhi, ISKCON Youth Forum: iyfdelhi.database@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
