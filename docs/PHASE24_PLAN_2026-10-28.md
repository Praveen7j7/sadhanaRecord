## Does not apply (decided before)

- **Capacitor:** store distribution is a Trusted Web Activity (Play) and a WKWebView shell (iOS) over the same PWA
  (docs/store-distribution-audit.md). No second build of the app.
- **A new framework, global store or data-fetching library:** the brief itself rules these out; the app is Svelte 5
  with its own small cache (lib/cache.svelte.js).
- **Glass effects and heavy animation:** the project's design rule is restrained visuals for low-end phones (no
  pause buttons on rotators, motion budget in the gate).
