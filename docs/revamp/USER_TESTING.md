# Sadhana Record — user testing kit

**IYF Delhi · for the person who runs the sessions · prepared 9 Oct 2026 (Phase 9, item 17)**

We want to see where real people get stuck, before everyone else does. Each session takes about 20 minutes, with one person at a time: **5 devotees and 2 coordinators**, chosen by IYF. Bring a mix: someone new to apps, someone older, someone who uses a small or old phone.

**The one rule:** you watch, you don't help. If they ask "what do I press?", say "What would you try?" Their hesitation is exactly what we need to see. Help only if they are stuck for more than a minute, and write down that you did.

---

## 1. Before the session

- **Their own phone, their own account,** on the live app (the address they normally use). Nothing is created just for the test.
- Signed out at the start (use **Log out** in the menu), so the sign-in is part of the test.
- Ask permission to take notes. No video or screen recording of their phone is needed.
- Have a printed observation sheet (§4) and a pen. Fill one sheet per person.
- Say this, in your own words:
  > "We are testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud: say what you are looking for and what you expect to happen. If something is confusing, that's the app's fault, and it helps us fix it."

## 2. Tasks for a devotee (about 15 minutes)

Read each task aloud as written. Don't name buttons or screens.

| # | Task (read aloud) | It went well if… |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | "Please sign in to the app." | Signed in without help, in under a minute. |
| D2 | "Fill in today's sadhana — what you have done so far today." | Today's day saved, and they noticed it saved. |
| D3 | "Suppose you forgot to fill in a day earlier this week. Fill it in now." | Found an earlier day of the week and saved it. |
| D4 | "How is your week going so far? Find out." | Found the week's progress or score. |
| D5 | "Find your report for the last few weeks." | Opened the report and could say one thing it shows. |
| D6 | "There is a message from IYF for everyone. Find it and like it." | Opened Announcements and liked a post. |
| D7 | Only if the submit window is open (the end of the week): "Send this week to your coordinator." | Submitted, and understood it was sent. |
| D8 | "Now log out." | Found Log out. |

## 3. Tasks for a coordinator (about 15 minutes)

| # | Task (read aloud) | It went well if… |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | "Please sign in." | Signed in without help. |
| C2 | "Who in your batch hasn't started this week yet? Show me." | Found the batch board and read the "not started" names. |
| C3 | Only if someone is waiting: "Someone has asked to join your batch. Look at their request and decide." | Opened Requests; approved or turned down with confidence. |
| C4 | "Mark last Sunday's class attendance for two devotees." | Marked present / absent and saw it saved. |
| C5 | "A devotee has no phone this week. Fill in one of their days for them." | Found "fill for", filled a day, and knew whose day it was. |
| C6 | "Find one devotee's details — their phone number or facilitator." | Opened the devotee and found it. |
| C7 | "Post a short message to your batch." (They can remove it right after.) | Posted to their batch only, and then removed it. |

## 5. Their own words (two minutes, at the end)

Ask, and write the answers down as said:

1. "On a scale of 1 to 5, how easy was it?" (1 = very hard, 5 = very easy) ____
2. "What was the most confusing moment?"
3. "What would you change first?"
4. "Would you fill it every day? Why, or why not?"

## 6. After all seven sessions

- Send the sheets (photos are fine) to the app team.
- The team lists every problem, counts how many people hit it, and fixes them in that order: what stopped someone first, then what slowed several people, then the rest.
- Each fix goes into the next release, which is tested end to end before it goes live.
