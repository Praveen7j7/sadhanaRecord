## What went live

1. **TRD temple activities** on the Day form: Mangal Aarti, Darshan Aarti, Morning Class, Prasadam, each On Time / Late /
   Absent. Only the Temple Residents batch; recorded, not scored, optional; in the staff day summary and the WhatsApp text.
2. **Hearing:** a strip of the 81 lesson weeks replaces ‹ ›; one search box over the whole journal (keeps its focus).
3. **Facilitator targets** (ADR 0018): a first visit opens on setting targets (the Yes/No measures can be set now; they
   never could, so they were never scored). Set targets are fixed after the phase's first week (or the date in Admin ›
   Phases); a never-set one may still be set; the head opens one person's for 7 days from their card, with a reason.
   Monthly measures show the month so far against the target.
4. **Promotion explorer** for facilitators: their students on the ladder, any batch, the batch rule applied, with ticks,
   thresholds of their own and criteria added from outside the rule; phase / last week / 4 / 52 weeks / custom.
5. **Admin › Analysis** for the head: every measure for a period against the one before, a chart per measure by batch,
   an increase/drop report, and the trainings (in it now, how it ended, attendance, card figures) on the same screen.
6. **Vaishnava Songs** in the menu for everyone: the English book (170 entries, searchable, stanzas and translations),
   both books as PDFs (the Hindi one only as a PDF: its text is in an old font encoding).
