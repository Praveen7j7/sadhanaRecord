## How it went out, and what went wrong before it

Earlier the same day five deploys went out with the raw `wrangler deploy`, around the gate: ETP enrolment fixes,
the IYF ID format, scoreboards, devices, the ETP sidebar. They passed the server and client suites and the browser
parts that were run, but the ledger stopped matching production, so the gate blocked the next release.

Reconciled with the gate's own command, not by hand: production's `index.html` (`index-D7qH12kY.js`) was fetched and
recorded as phase 17 with `gate.mjs record 17 --dist`, which re-ran the live smoke checks first. The previous ledger is
kept at `impl/baseline/phases-ledger-before-2026-10-18.json`.

From this release on (the head's policy, `CLAUDE.md` §Releasing): **bulk changes go through the full gate; patches go
out after targeted tests of the changed piece, its parent, its children and its siblings.**
