# Sadhana Record — promo video scripts

**IYF Delhi · 10 October 2026 · two videos, narrated by a person (not a synthesiser)**

**Why a person reads these.** The walkthrough videos use the Mac's built-in `say` voice. It has no sentence-level
rhythm, so every line lands the same way — the "robotic" delivery IYF rejected. No setting fixes that; it is the
engine. These two scripts are therefore written **to be read aloud by a devotee**, and the timings assume a human.

**Who should read it:** a male voice, Indian English, clear and unhurried — the accent Delhi youth hear every day,
not an imitation British or American one. Someone who can say *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* and *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*
properly, which no synthesiser does.

**How to record** (no studio needed):
- a phone, in a small carpeted room, at night; duvet or curtains behind the reader kill the echo;
- hold the phone a hand's width away, slightly off to the side (stops the hard "p" and "b" pops);
- **one chapter per clip**, named `01.m4a`, `02.m4a` …, so a fluffed line means re-reading one chapter, not all of it;
- read a touch slower than feels natural, and **pause a full second** at each `//` mark;
- read it twice and keep the second take: the first is always stiff.

**The golden rule for the reader:** this is not an advertisement. It is one devotee explaining something useful to
another. Warm, plain, unhurried. No salesmanship, no exclamation.

## Making the videos

1. **Record the voice first.** The visuals are cut to the narration, never the other way round.
2. **Capture the app b-roll** with the existing tool on demo data (`tools/video`, mock API, port 5199) — no real
   devotee appears and no Cloudflare quota is used.
3. **Assemble** with the title cards and number cards over the b-roll.
4. **Before sharing:** watch both the whole way through with the sound on. Check no real name or phone number is
   visible in any frame.
