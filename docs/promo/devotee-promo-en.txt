--- 01 ---
Every day, a devotee makes small promises.  To rise early. To chant attentively. To read. To serve.

--- 02 ---
And every day, those promises are easy to lose track of.  Not from a lack of sincerity — simply because a week is long, and memory is short.

--- 03 ---
Sadhana Record is IYF Delhi's own app for keeping that record.

--- 04 ---
You fill your day in under a minute. Chanting. Hearing. Reading. Service. Rest.  No accounts to reconcile, no spreadsheet to find.

--- 05 ---
If the signal drops on the metro, your entry waits on your phone and sends itself when you are back online.  Nothing is lost.

--- 06 ---
At the end of the week, you see where you actually stand —  not a score to be judged by, but a mirror.

--- 07 ---
The days you missed are shown plainly, in amber, not in red.  This is a record, not a report card.

--- 08 ---
Your facilitator sees the same picture, so when you are struggling, someone notices —  and can reach out before a difficult week becomes a difficult month.

--- 09 ---
And each day the app opens with a darshan, and a verse from Śrīla Prabhupāda's books —  a small reminder of why any of this is being recorded at all.

--- 10 ---
It works on any phone, installs like an app, and opens in under two seconds —  even on an older handset, even on a slow connection.

--- 11 ---
Your details stay with IYF Delhi. No advertising, no tracking, no third parties.

--- 12 ---
Sadhana Record.  Keep the promise. Remember the day.
